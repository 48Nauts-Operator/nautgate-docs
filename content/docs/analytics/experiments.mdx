---
title: Experiments
description: Champion–challenger trials on your own traffic.
---

A sampled slice of real calls is answered a second time in the background by a
challenger, and a blind judge decides whether anyone would have noticed.

## Guarantees

- Never touches the response path — the trial runs after the real answer shipped.
- Prompts classified `secret` are never mirrored.
- Only tool-free calls with a captured prompt and a non-empty champion answer are
  eligible.
- A combined daily cost cap covers challenger and judge calls together.
- Failures are recorded as errors rather than silently dropped.

## Two kinds of trial

**Model** — a cheaper model answers the same prompt.

**Prompt diet** — the *same* model answers a pruned prompt, recording original and
pruned byte counts. A diet strategy that proves out can then be applied in-flight
to a given agent's real requests.

## Judging

Blind and pairwise. The judge sees "Answer 1" and "Answer 2" without knowing which
is which, at temperature 0, and returns a winner or a tie.

## The statistics

Challenger wins plus ties count as acceptable. That rate is tested against a
**one-sided binomial non-inferiority test at p₀ = 0.90**, requiring at least ten
trials before it will call a result. The claim being tested is "the cheaper model
is not meaningfully worse", not "the cheaper model is better".

<Callout>
`projected_monthly_saving_usd` is a **linear extrapolation** — the per-call cost
difference multiplied by the champion's 30-day volume. It assumes the mix of work
stays the same. Treat it as a scale indicator, not a forecast.
</Callout>
