Aggregates over the audit log for a window, filterable by agent and project:
calls, cost, notional cost, tokens, average latency and empty-response counts,
broken down by provider, model, tier, agent and project.

## Subscription saved

The headline number. It is `SUM(notional_cost_usd)` — **the metered list price of
traffic your flat-rate plans covered**, computed per call at the same prices a
metered call would have used.

<Callout>
This is not money saved by routing. It is what the same work would have been
billed at through the API, for calls that your subscription made free. It is a
"was the plan worth it" number, not an optimisation result.
</Callout>

Throughout the analytics, spend is summed as
`COALESCE(NULLIF(cost_usd, 0), notional_cost_usd, 0)` — real metered cost where
billed, otherwise the notional value — so subscription traffic is not invisible
in an agent's totals.

## OpenRouter balance

`GET /v1/cost/openrouter-balance` reports remaining credit and a seven-day local
burn rate. It is the one cost view that makes an outbound call, and it respects
[offline mode](/docs/operations/offline-mode).

## Budgets

Per project, agent or model family, daily or monthly, with a warning threshold.
Exceeding a cap returns HTTP 429.
